# Study of clinical data and its relation to Heart Stroke in 40901 inpatients

This study was based on a real dataset obtained from Kaggle, here, we use AI (TabPFN model) to understand the rationale behind the clinical data and the disease.
The model is publicly available at https://github.com/PriorLabs/tabpfn and TabPFN Client is available at https://github.com/priorlabs/tabpfn-client
